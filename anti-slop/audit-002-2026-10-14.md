# Anti-slop audit 002

Date: 2026-10-14
Scope: sticky header logo sizing at wide desktop widths
Mode: after

## Finding

1. **Oversized wide-desktop header and logo** (HIGH)

   `public/assets/makeover.css` applies a `30rem` header height at widths of `80rem` and up, then expands the horizontal logo to `min(72rem, 100%)`. This makes the navigation header dominate the page and pushes the content far below the fold. It violates R-03 (responsive layout), R-05 (rhythm and hierarchy), R-35 (verify across widths), and C-4 (resilience).

   Proposed fix: remove the wide-desktop masthead override, keep the header in its compact navigation state, and give the horizontal lockup a controlled responsive maximum so the logo remains prominent without becoming the page.

## Approval gate

No UI files or deployment were changed by this audit. Approve finding `1` to apply the sizing fix and verify desktop plus narrow layouts.

## Resolution

Finding 1 approved and fixed. The wide-desktop masthead override was removed, the desktop header/logo now use a fluid compact scale, and the stylesheet URL was versioned to clear the stale browser cache. Validation passed, the live desktop header measured about 89px high with a 68px logo, and the 390px mobile check measured a 52px logo. Deployed as Worker version `2c322da0-ff85-4a13-9e06-02aa906dcc38`.
