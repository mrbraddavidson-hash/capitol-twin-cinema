# Anti-slop audit 004

Date: 2026-10-14
Scope: admin panel first-run hierarchy and task flow
Mode: after

## Finding

1. **Secondary tools compete with the publishing workflow** (MEDIUM)

   After sign-in, the live admin view places the Facebook source checker, the two-screen search, the full Screen Listing form, Live Listings, an eight-item brand-kit gallery with an embedded asset browser, and Advanced settings in one long sequence. The actual recurring decision is narrower: choose the two films, enter their times, and publish the two listings. The full-width source and brand sections push that work down and make the panel feel like a catalogue instead of a focused theatre tool. This conflicts with C-3 (content-driven composition), R-20 (build an app screen around its primary decision), and R-03 (responsive resilience).

   Proposed fix: keep Screen Listing and Live Listings immediately visible, and make Facebook updates plus the brand kit on-demand collapsed panels with clear summaries. Preserve every existing control, ID, API call, asset link, and the already-collapsed Advanced settings panel. This reduces first-run noise without removing capability.

## Approval gate

No UI files or deployment were changed by this audit. Approve finding `1` to apply the focused hierarchy change and verify the signed-in admin view at desktop and narrow widths.

## Resolution

Finding 1 approved and fixed. Live Listings now appears first after sign-in, followed by Screen Listing. Facebook Updates and the Brand Kit are native collapsed panels so their existing controls remain available without crowding the publishing workflow; Advanced settings remains collapsed. The live admin view was verified in the browser at desktop size and at a 390 × 844 mobile viewport, including the new order and responsive card flow. Deployed as Worker version `3d3bb938-5470-4b88-ab48-5ee63494f5be`.
